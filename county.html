Hickston County

Four chapters, one county

The notes name Hickston, Possum County Fair, Blackwater Timber, and Stillwater Cut. County Crossroads connects them. It is not a fifth chapter.

Chapter 01 · The Hickston Incident

Hickston

Start at Stanky's, search dispatch and service roads for the way into the barn and pasture, and rescue Bessie. Roadside businesses, county backlots and barnyard trouble lead to the Sheriff's showdown.

Chapter 02

Possum County Fair

An abandoned midway still has a broadcast to silence. Find the patch lead, restore the backstage PA connection and reach the pavilion. Prize stalls become cover when Rudy Racket takes the stage with his charged horn.

Chapter 03

Blackwater Timber

Work through wet timber yards, feed controls and the dry kiln to shut down the pump-house relay. Log stacks offer cover, chain hands punish careless approaches, and the Splinter Foreman marks cutting lanes through the finale.

Chapter 04 · The Bootleg Works

Stillwater Cut

Limestone processing meets copper condensers and a contaminated bottling operation. Isolate the cutting feed, work the condenser and vent the relay before clearing the loading court. Watch Boilerback gauges, Chalk Spitter marks and the Copper Baron's pressure outlets.

Hub, not a fifth chapter

County Crossroads

Four road gates link the campaign. Continue in campaign order, suspend a chapter at its return gate, or revisit a completed world. Gate prompts distinguish locked destinations, continuing, revisiting and replay state.
