# T6 LOOKS: notes for the guide (pass 16)

What the looks layer can do since pass 16, for T10 to fold into
MAP_AUTHORING.md (and for T7, the source compiler, and T8/T9, the player
map and the MAP SHED, to call). Everything here is **opt-in per map**: no
chapter uses any of it, and a map that says nothing looks and plays as
before.

## The one call

The looks go over a level that is already built (a plan's or a source's
finished level dictionary, or a player map's `UserMapBuild.level()`):

```gdscript
var report := MapLooks.apply(level, {
    "mood": "farm_dusk",
    "areas": [{"name": "THE BARN", "rect": [41, 7, 51, 14], "mood": "barn_dark"}],
    "water": [{"type": "creek", "points": [[1, 23], [26, 22.5], [62, 24]], "width": 1.8}],
    "set_pieces": [{"kind": "tower", "id": "tank", "at": [57.5, 13.5], "falls": [1, 3]}],
    "dress": [{"theme": "farm", "area": [3, 3, 20, 18], "count": 2, "seed": 2}],
    "foliage": [{"kind": "grass", "area": [1, 1, 62, 46], "density": .3, "seed": 8}],
})
```

(`src/map_kit/map_looks.gd`.) It runs in this order: areas and moods, water,
set pieces, explicit vignettes, dress, foliage. Water goes first so the
plants and the dressing see it; foliage goes last so it keeps clear of
everything else. The report is `{"problems": [[code, line]...], "vignettes":
[...], "plants": n, "water_cells": n, "ms": {...}}`. `vignettes` holds the
vignette objects the dress objects chose; write them back into the source to
fix them in place. A whole demo is `godot/tools/looks_demo.gd`, a 64 x 48
farm.

In a map source (T7 wires these in as types): `foliage`, `dress`,
`vignette`, `pond`, `creek`, `trough`, `set_piece`, plus a district's (or
the map's) `mood`. Each object below is written the way a source would
write it. In `MapLooks.apply`, drop `type` and put the object in its list.

## Moods by area

A mood is a preset in `godot/data/tuning/effects.cfg` `[mood.<name>]`. It
sets fog (its colour, where it starts, where it is full, how thick at most),
the grade (the tint on every surface, and so the light's colour), fog banks,
how much of the rain is drawn, crows and fireflies. It can also set a room
sound and a far-off cue for its district. The presets:

| mood | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| `farm_dusk` | warm low sun, dusty orange fog, crows, fireflies, a dog far off |
| `barn_dark` | inside a barn: close, brown, dim, no rain drawn, the barn's echo |
| `church_still` | a white church: pale, cool, quiet |
| `holler_fog` | grey fog low and thick, fog banks, crows far off |
| `bayou_night` | green-black murk, fireflies, the creek's sound |
| `store_fluorescent` | a store or an office: flat green-white light, little fog |
| `mothership_glow` | teal haze, cold bright light |

```json
{"mood": "farm_dusk"}
{"areas": [{"name": "THE WOODS", "rect": [1, 29, 14, 46], "mood": "holler_fog"}]}
{"area_moods": {"THE BARN": "barn_dark"}}
```

- `mood`: the whole map's. A district with no mood of its own takes it, and
  a map with no `mood` keeps its own effects.cfg `[level.<id>]` values.
- `areas`: a new district over a rectangle of cells (both corners in),
  named for the HUD and the map, with an optional mood and `tint` [r, g, b].
- `area_moods`: a mood for districts that already exist, by name or index.
- As the player crosses into a district with another mood, the view eases
  to it over `[moods] blend` (1.5 s). A jump of more than `snap` (3 cells),
  such as a respawn, takes the new mood at once.
- Looks only: nothing is carried online or saved. The level file holds
  `"mood"` and `"area_moods": {"<district index>": "<mood>"}`. A mood's
  `sound` and `cue` are written into `area_sounds` and `area_cues` for its
  district, unless the map names its own.
- A new preset is a new `[mood.<name>]` section. Leave out any key that
  should stay the level's own.

## Foliage

```json
{"type": "foliage", "kind": "pine", "area": [1, 29, 14, 46], "density": 1, "seed": 2}
{"type": "foliage", "kind": "corn", "area": [18, 20, 27, 28], "density": 1, "rows": true}
{"type": "foliage", "kind": "reeds", "polygon": [[2, 18], [40, 20], [40, 30], [2, 28]], "density": .3}
```

| kind | what | tall | blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `grass` | a tuft of pasture grass | .26-.42 | no |
| `weeds` | goldenrod or bull thistle | .42-.72 | no |
| `scrub` | a scrub bush (one dry and berried) | .7-1.1 | no |
| `tree` | the round leafy tree | 3.2-4.2 | trunk |
| `pine` | a loblolly pine | 3.8-5.2 | trunk |
| `cypress` | a bald cypress (grows in bayou water too) | 3.6-4.8 | trunk |
| `snag` | a dead snag | 2.8-3.4 | trunk |
| `corn` | a hill of corn (on dirt and grass) | 1.7-2.1 | no |
| `reeds` | reeds, only within 1.6 of water | .5-.78 | no |

- `area` [x0, y0, x1, y1] in cells (both corners in), or `polygon` of map
  points, or neither (the whole map). `density` 0 to 1 (default 1): the
  spacing between two plants of a kind is its `spacing` over the density's
  square root. `seed`: the same object gives the same plants on every
  machine; another seed gives others. `rows: true` plants in rows (corn).
- Where a plant may go: on its kind's ground (the natural floors `grass`,
  `dirt`, `orchard`, `bayou_mud`, `bayou_bank`, `bayou_marsh`; each kind's
  `ground` in `dressing.json`); out in the open (no roof, no deck or ceiling
  over it); off cliffs; and clear of all of these:
  - 1.5 from the way through. The way is worked out from the start to every
    objective down the middle of the open ground, plus a versus map's routes.
  - 1.5 from a door, an objective (station, key, exit, gate, vehicle, set
    piece, a versus site) or a spawn.
  - 1.5 from a road floor (`road`, `asphalt`, `gravel`, `tracks`, `shoulder`
    and the rest in `dressing.json` "roads").
  - .8 from a hand or a pickup, .6 from a sign or another prop, the kind's
    `wall` distance from a wall, and furniture.
- Plants are camera-facing sprites drawn by the sprite layer, with no node
  of their own. A trunk gets a low base that stops a body; the others are
  walked through.
- Budget (`dressing.json` "budget"): at most **360 plants a map** and **16 in
  any 6 x 6 cells**. Past that a plant is left out (`foliage_budget`). This
  keeps the sprites on screen under County Run's busiest frame (252); see
  "Measured" below.

## Dressing: vignettes

A vignette is a hand-made group of things that belong together, set with its
back to a wall. Nothing is ever scattered to fill ground (the owner's rule).

```json
{"type": "dress", "area": [3, 3, 20, 18], "theme": "farm", "count": 2, "seed": 2}
{"type": "vignette", "id": "porch_sittin", "at": [12, 14], "wall": "n"}
```

- `dress` picks `count` (1 to 12) vignettes of the theme and their spots from
  its seed. Each goes against a wall in the area (a solid cell, or a thin
  geometry wall along the cell's edge), on flat open floor that is not a
  road, water or terrain. Each is clear of the way through (1.5), doors,
  objectives, spawns and things, at least 4 cells from another vignette, and
  never where its solid items would shut off ground the start could reach.
  Inside ones go under a roof, outside ones under the sky. A `by_door` one
  wants a door within 4 cells.
- `vignette` stands one where it is told. `at` is the cell at its corner
  against the wall; `wall` is the side the wall is on (`n`, `e`, `s`, `w`).
  It runs along the wall (east for n and s, south for w and e) and out from
  it.
- Themes: `farm`, `junk`, `church`, `fair`, `yard_sale`, `shine`, `garage`,
  `swamp`, `house`, `store`.

| vignette | themes | where | size | what |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `porch_sittin` | farm, house, yard_sale | outside, by a door | 4 x 2 | porch couch, cooler, rocker, bug zapper |
| `junk_pile` | junk, farm, garage | outside | 4 x 2 | tyre pile, busted fridge, drum, tyre stack |
| `wash_day` | house, farm, yard_sale | outside | 4 x 2 | laundry line, washer, bathtub |
| `wood_pile` | farm, house, swamp | outside | 4 x 2 | log pile, chopping stump, wheelbarrow |
| `stock_corner` | farm | outside | 4 x 2 | a water trough, hay bales stacked |
| `chicken_yard` | farm, house | outside | 3 x 2 | chicken coop, wheelbarrow |
| `church_yard` | church | outside | 5 x 2 | four headstones, a lantern post |
| `church_social` | church, fair | outside, by a door | 4 x 2 | picnic table, ice chest, grill |
| `fair_corner` | fair, yard_sale | outside | 4 x 2 | camp chairs, cooler, gnome, flamingo |
| `yard_sale` | yard_sale, junk | outside | 5 x 2 | table, gnome, mailboxes, flamingos |
| `shine_nook` | shine, swamp | any | 4 x 2 | still, mash barrel, jugs |
| `tinker_corner` | garage, junk | any | 4 x 2 | engine stand, tool chest, gas cans, tyres |
| `bait_shack` | swamp | outside | 4 x 2 | crawfish traps, net rack, outboard stand |
| `back_room` | house | inside | 4 x 2 | bunk, strongbox, ice chest |
| `store_back` | store, garage | inside | 4 x 2 | pallets, jugs, gas cans, open safe |

- A new vignette goes in `dressing.json` "vignettes". Each item is a town
  prop (`prop` with a model in `assets3d/props/meshes.json`, `at`
  [along, out] from the corner, `solid` half width of a base that blocks,
  `scale`, `z`) or a fixture (`box` [along, out] half sizes, `top`, `side`,
  `cap`; the trough is a `tin` box with a `creek` cap).
- A prop whose picture `breakables.cfg` lists (barrels, coolers, crates, gas
  cans...) breaks in play wherever a vignette stands it.

## Water

```json
{"type": "pond", "at": [22.5, 37.5], "radius": 3.7}
{"type": "pond", "polygon": [[20, 10], [27, 9], [29, 15], [21, 16]], "deep": false}
{"type": "creek", "points": [[1, 23], [14, 24.5], [26, 22.5], [62, 24]], "width": 1.8}
{"type": "trough", "at": [37, 12.5], "along": "y", "length": 2}
```

- A pond takes `polygon`, `at` + `radius`, or `rect` [x0, y0, x1, y1]. Its
  water lies flat at its lowest bank less the bed (`depth`, default .35).
  Cells 2 or more from dry ground drop to deep water (`bayou_deep`, nobody
  wades it) at .8 unless `"deep": false`.
- A creek runs along `points`, `width` wide (at least one cell, unbroken).
  Its water follows the ground down. Where it crosses a road floor a plank
  bridge goes over, a hair above the road and railed on the two sides over
  the water.
- Both carve a terrain bed (T1 terrain, geometry version 2, written only
  when used). The shore (the dry cells round the water) slopes down to it in
  `shore` (default `bayou_bank`; `""` keeps the floor). The water is `tile`
  (default `creek`; `bayou_water` for a swamp). Each carved cell's ground is
  its lowest corner.
- Nothing is drowned. A cell with a door, a station, the exit, a spawn, a
  hand, a pickup, a sign or furniture in it stays dry (`water_thing`), and
  so do cells under a roof or a deck. A pond does not cross a road.
- A trough is a fixture: tin sides, water in it, .45 high, .8 to 4 long.

## Set pieces

```json
{"type": "set_piece", "kind": "barn", "id": "hay_shed", "at": [30.5, 12.6], "turn": 2, "falls": [0, 1]}
{"type": "set_piece", "kind": "tower", "id": "tank", "at": [57.5, 13.5], "falls": [1, 3]}
{"type": "set_piece", "kind": "fuel", "id": "line", "points": [[10, 30], [18, 30]], "pumps": [[10.6, 29.4]]}
```

- These are the chapters' big breaks, played by `features/set_pieces.gd`. A
  pole barn comes down when its posts are shot out. A water tower goes over
  and floods the ground. A fuel line leaks and burns. Their sizes and
  numbers are in `set_pieces.cfg`. A barn may also carry `w`, `d`, `eave`,
  `wall_tile`, `roof_tile`; a tower `sign`, `sign_face`.
- `falls`: the ways it may go over (0 east, 1 south, 2 west, 3 north).
- The footprint must be open ground under the sky, off the start, the doors
  and the objectives (`set_piece_spot`, and it is left out). A fall that
  would land on a wall says `set_piece_fall`: drop that way from `falls`.
- `godot --headless --path godot --script res://tools/set_piece_check.gd --
  level=<built level.json>` (or `-- <id>`) brings a map's set pieces down
  every way and checks the start still reaches everything.
- Windows break by tile (any `_win` wall tile), and plank and fence walls
  break as the chapters' do. Neither needs a hook.

## Problem codes (one plain line each)

| code | means | what happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mood_unknown` | no such `[mood.*]` | left out |
| `mood_area` | `area_moods` names no district | left out |
| `area_rect` | an area without a 4-number rect | left out |
| `foliage_kind` | no such foliage kind | left out |
| `foliage_area` | the area is off the map | left out |
| `foliage_none` | no room in the area (no natural ground in the open, clear of things) | nothing placed |
| `foliage_budget` | the map's 360 plants reached | the rest left out |
| `dress_theme` | no such theme | left out |
| `dress_none` / `dress_short` | no wall with room / fewer than asked | what fits is placed |
| `vignette` | no such vignette, or a bad wall side | left out |
| `water_type` | not pond, creek or trough | left out |
| `water_shape` | a creek with under two points, a trough off the floor | left out |
| `water_thing` | cells left dry for what stands in them | the rest carved |
| `water_none` | nothing could be carved | left out |
| `set_piece_kind` / `set_piece_spot` | bad kind / footprint not clear | left out |
| `set_piece_fall` | a fall lands on a wall | added, fix `falls` |
| `looks_blocked` | the looks shut the way to an objective | added; move what blocks |

## Measured (this PC, 2026-11-27)

- `MapLooks.apply` on the demo: about 0.75 s headless. Of that, foliage is
  about 0.4 s, water 0.11 s and the way worked out twice 0.12 s.
- `stress_bench only=looks` (a window, 1280 x 800): the dense demo at its
  360-plant budget with 6 hands awake, standing where the most plants are in
  view. 244 sprites listed (225 of them plants), against County Run's 252.
  Frame median 2.2 to 3.7 ms over 4 runs, against county's 13.2 and 14.8 ms
  in the same runs.

## For the other tracks

- **T7 (source v2):** compile the level as now, then call
  `MapLooks.apply(level, looks)` with the source's looks objects gathered
  into its lists. `report["problems"]` become validate findings, each with
  the object's id. A `dress` object's `report["vignettes"]` can be offered
  as `vignette` objects to pin them. Districts in the source may carry
  `mood`, so pass them as `area_moods` by name.
- **T8 (player map v2):** `areas` with a mood map to `MapLooks.add_area` and
  `moods`. Foliage and vignettes are compiled the same way at runtime, since
  everything in `src/map_kit/` is typed and has no clock or dice. The MAP
  SHED's AUTO-DRESS calls `MapDressing.place` and writes its vignette
  objects into the map as things.
- **T2 (map check):** a map with `set_pieces` can run `set_piece_check --
  level=<its level.json>`. The review's "looks" line can count districts
  without a mood.
- **New tiles:** none. The 14 new pictures (`foliage_*`) are sprites in the
  base art pack, made by `tools/art/build_foliage_art.gd`.

## Limits (said plainly)

- Vignettes go on flat ground only, not on terrain.
- Plants grow only on the ground floor: not on decks or roofs.
- The way through is worked out on the grid at ground level (thin walls
  count, decks do not), so an objective up on a floor is treated as reached
  through the cell under it.
- A mood cannot change the sky picture or the level's music. The level's
  score and sky stay one a map.
- Water is still a floor tile. A bed gives it depth to look at; nobody
  swims (deep water stops a body as the bayou's does).
- A pond's water is flat and a creek's follows the ground, cell corner by
  cell corner. Under a creek on a steep slope the water slopes too.
