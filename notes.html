Project notes

Notes and credits

The copy on this site follows the reviewed 1.4.3 project notes. The public download is the v1.4.3 prerelease ZIP.

Version

Reviewed project notes and public prerelease: 1.4.3. It is a Windows x64 development build.

The download button uses the ZIP published on the v1.4.3 release. It does not point at a later build.

Download the ZIP

Music

The notes describe twelve original chapter arrangements, three per location, with shuffled playlists. Title, jukebox, and finale music are separate.

This website does not play a soundtrack preview.

Credits

Crazy Legs Mundell Rampage is a game by Treymun.

An independent, unofficial game project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators or publishers of Redneck Rampage. This site does not use that game's logo and is not an official sequel.

Website material textures: Poly Haven, CC0. The three scans on the media page are production material textures, not gameplay pictures.

The game uses other third-party work. Those notices stay with the game package and should not be stripped out of it. This website does not claim every game asset is wholly original, and it does not publish private portraits.

The gameplay stills and silent clips are scripted captures from the published 1.4.3 source renderer. A harness called the game's walk, turn, and fire controls on Linux. They are not Windows recordings, and they have no audio.
