Twelve weapons

An eccentric arsenal

From crowbar and sawed-off to a Bottle Rocket Launcher, Mill Riveter and Copperhead Pressure Gun, choose the right tool for the trouble ahead.

Named in the notes

The notes describe twelve weapons. The names below are the ones called out for this site. Keys 1 through 8 select other weapons, and those names are not listed here.

  • Crowbar
  • Sawed-off
  • Sling BladeSelect with 9
  • Bottle Rocket LauncherSelect with 0
  • Mill RiveterSelect with -
  • Copperhead Pressure GunSelect with =

Supplies

Pork rinds, beer, and moonshine are carried supplies, not part of the twelve-weapon count. The default keys are C, B, and M.
